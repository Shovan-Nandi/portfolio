The engineer behind the infrastructure

I'm a backend engineer with 8 years of professional experience — 7 spent building secure, scalable systems with PHP 8, Laravel, Livewire and Filament, and the last 2.7 designing and operating the production cloud infrastructure those systems run on. I've provisioned and managed EC2, S3, RDS, Elastic Load Balancer, ElastiCache and Route 53 to run high-traffic, multi-tenant applications, including a 16-site community platform built entirely on AWS.

I also maintain two open-source Filament plugins listed in the official plugins directory.
